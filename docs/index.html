Privacy Policy

Focus App  ·  Last updated: June 2026

🔒

The short version

Focus does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. Everything stays on your device. We have no servers, no accounts, no tracking.

📦

What data we collect

None. Focus does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No analytics or crash reporting SDKs
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No location, contacts, or media access
📱

Data stored on your device

Focus stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • List of apps you have added to Focus
  • Focus session history (app name, duration, timestamp)
  • Your theme preference (dark / light mode)

This data never leaves your device. It is stored in the app's private storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

🔌

VPN usage

Focus uses Android's VPN API solely to block internet access for selected apps on your device. This is a local tunnel — no traffic is routed through any external server. We do not inspect, log, or transmit any network traffic.

📋

Permissions explained

  • BIND_VPN_SERVICE — Required to create the local VPN tunnel that blocks internet for selected apps
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — Required to keep the VPN active while the selected app is running
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required to show the persistent VPN status notification on Android 13+
  • INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — Required by the VPN API
👶

Children's privacy

Focus does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Since we collect no data at all, this is inherently satisfied.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, the revised version will be posted on this page with an updated date. Since Focus is open source, all changes are publicly visible in the repository.
